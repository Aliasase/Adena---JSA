SIL-PKG-004 — Architect Response 001
Reconciliation
Accept the general reconciliation approach, including canonical hazard consolidation, control reuse and immutable identifiers.
Final record-level acceptance remains pending review of the reconciled library and proposed changes P-1 through P-5.
Electrical Exposure
Distinguish a required work-zone electrical assessment from confirmed electrical exposure.
For applicable mobile crane operations, anticipate potential overhead electrical exposure unless the context establishes otherwise.
When overhead power lines are identified, prioritize electrical contact and arcing hazards.
Do not apply mobile crane electrical clearance controls to ordinary overhead bridge crane operations. Preserve equipment-specific electrical exposures.
Tag Lines
Recognize applicable MSHA requirements for loads requiring steadying or guidance.
Do not establish a universal requirement that every suspended load must have a tag line.
Account for electrical conductivity, entanglement, recoil, personnel positioning and load-control effectiveness.
Beam Clamp Recognition
Accept the four distinct applications:

* Clamps gripping material being lifted.
* Beam clamps and trolleys supporting hoists.
* Temporary fall protection anchorage connectors.
* Structural and mechanical fastening hardware.

Maintain cumulative applicability when multiple devices are used.
For hoist-supporting beam clamps and trolleys, recognize supporting structure capacity, attachment compatibility, loading direction, unintended travel, disengagement and applicable end-of-travel protection.
Multiple-Lift Rigging
Expand the package to recognize multiple-lift rigging, Christmas-treeing and multiple-member steel erection lifts.
Address:

* Multiple suspended structural members.
* Rigging assembly capacity.
* Individual attachment capacity.
* Total load weight.
* Load distribution.
* Member spacing.
* Member collision.
* Controlled placement sequence.
* Personnel exposure beneath remaining suspended members.
* Stability of previously placed members.
* Premature rigging release.

Develop corresponding controls addressing approved multiple-lift rigging assemblies, equipment suitability, qualified personnel, attachment configuration, controlled placement sequence and applicable restrictions.
Verify all numerical limitations and steel-erection-specific requirements before implementation.
Distinguish multiple-lift rigging from tandem crane lifts and ordinary multi-leg sling configurations.
Personnel Hoisting
Retain personnel-hoisting recognition and applicable hazard records.
Withhold unvalidated personnel-hoisting-specific controls pending technical and regulatory validation.
Do not suppress independently applicable and previously validated controls.
Do not imply that recognition of a personnel-hoisting task establishes authorization to perform it.
Evaluate steel-erection-specific provisions separately.

Additional Regression Requirements
Include:

* Christmas-treeing structural steel.
* Multiple-lift rigging assembly.
* Tandem crane lift.
* Single load using a four-leg sling.
* Install beam clamp to support chain hoist.
* Install trolley for overhead hoist.
* Use beamers while receiving suspended steel.
* Lift near energized overhead power lines.
* Operate overhead bridge crane indoors.
* Mobile crane setup with no overhead electrical lines present.
* Guide suspended load at an MSHA-regulated surface mine.
* Crane-suspended personnel platform during steel erection.

Preserve positive, negative and mixed-operation assertions.
No implementation or deployment is authorized by this disposition.
