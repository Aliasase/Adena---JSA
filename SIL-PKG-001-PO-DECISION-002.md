SIL-PKG-001 — PRODUCT OWNER DECISION RECORD 002
Company Safety Requirements Clarification
The Product Owner has confirmed the following Adena Corporation safety requirements.
These decisions supersede conflicting provisions in Product Owner Decision Record 001 and Architect Response 003.
1. Hand Protection
Adena requires general-purpose work gloves when working with steel.
Minimum ANSI A4 cut-resistant gloves are required for:

* All demolition activities.
* Knife use.
* Working with stainless steel lath (expanded metal).

These are company-wide requirements and shall be incorporated into the applicable canonical controls and hazard relationships.
Do not apply the A4 requirement universally to all steel work.
Recognize overlapping activities cumulatively. Where general steel work also involves demolition, knife use or stainless steel lath, the more protective applicable glove requirement governs.
Additional task-specific, chemical, thermal, electrical and mechanical hand protection requirements remain applicable.
2. Fall Protection
Adena's general fall protection trigger is six feet.
Where client, project or applicable regulatory requirements establish a lower threshold, the more protective requirement governs.
The six-foot threshold shall remain within the safety intelligence library.
Recommended canonical control:
"Use required fall protection when exposed to a fall hazard of 6 feet or greater, or at a lower height where required."
The intelligence shall also recognize circumstances requiring fall protection regardless of height.
Existing requirements for tie-off in both boom lifts and scissor lifts remain unchanged.
Do not introduce less protective steel erection exceptions.
3. Hearing Protection
Adena requires hearing protection at noise exposure levels of 85 dBA or greater.
Retain the numerical threshold as a company requirement rather than attributing it universally to a regulatory standard.
Recommended canonical control:
"Wear hearing protection when exposed to noise levels of 85 dBA or greater, in designated hearing protection areas, or when required for the task."
Task-specific noise controls shall remain applicable even when measured sound levels are unavailable.
Do not require employees to independently measure sound levels before selecting hearing protection.
4. Production Crosswalk
Reevaluate U-14.
The existing production six-foot fall protection and 85 dBA hearing protection thresholds are confirmed company requirements and are not policy conflicts.
Retain both thresholds.
Any proposed wording improvements shall preserve these requirements.
5. Permanent Policy Relationships
Establish these requirements as Product Owner-confirmed company policy records within the Safety Intelligence Library.
Link applicable canonical controls to these requirements across all current and future packages.
Do not duplicate canonical controls unnecessarily.
Retain the distinction between company policy, regulatory requirements, client requirements, technical specifications and manufacturer instructions.
6. Engineer Action

* Incorporate these decisions into Package 001 r3.
* Update the canonical control registry and production crosswalk.
* Resolve U-14 as a policy conflict.
* Resolve the identified hand protection portion of U-9.
* Preserve this decision record in permanent version-controlled documentation.
* Continue development of Package 002 — Welding and Hot Work.

No application implementation or production release is authorized.
