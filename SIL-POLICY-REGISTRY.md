# Safety Intelligence Library — Company Policy Registry

**Rules:**
- Identifiers use the format `P-000001` and run sequentially. They are permanent, are never changed, and are never reused after retirement.
- A policy record states a **company requirement**. It is kept distinct from regulatory requirements, client requirements, technical specifications, manufacturer instructions and best practices.
- A policy record is never attributed to a regulation, and a regulation is never presented as company policy.
- Policy records do not duplicate controls. They **link to canonical controls** in `SIL-CONTROL-REGISTRY.md`, and those controls cite the policy record in their internal basis.
- **More protective governs, for the same exposure.** Where a client, project or regulatory requirement is more protective than a policy record *for the same exposure*, it applies. Where overlapping activities trigger requirements addressing the **same exposure mechanism** (for example, work gloves vs. A4 cut-resistant gloves for cut exposure), the more protective one governs, and the less protective one is not presented as an alternative.
- **Different exposure mechanisms are cumulative** (PO Decision 003 §6). Requirements that address different mechanisms all apply. Cut resistance does not replace thermal protection. Thermal protection does not establish A4 cut resistance. Welding during demolition must meet both hand-protection requirements. Chemical, electrical, thermal and mechanical protection are evaluated independently where those exposures coexist.
- Only the Product Owner adds, changes or retires a policy record.

**Hot work scope (PO Decision 004 / Response 004 §1, P2-U1).** All ignition-producing grinding and abrasive cutting is hot work, including in fabrication shops; P-000006 to P-000008 apply. Non-ignition-producing grinding or mechanical cutting (for example, concrete grinding or cold-cutting) is not classified as hot work solely because Grind or Cut is recognized.

**Induction and resistance heating (SIL PO Decision 005 §2, closes P2-U16; corrected by Architect Response 005 §10).** Induction and resistance heating are hot work when the process creates an ignition hazard; P-000006 to P-000008 then apply. The absence of an open flame or sparks does not establish the absence of an ignition hazard. **Removing or protecting combustibles is a hot work control, not proof that the process has stopped being hot work** — phrases such as "area cleared of combustibles" never suppress the permit, clearance or fire watch. Only where ignition potential is genuinely absent is hot work not established. Where it is uncertain, applicability stays conditional. Thermal, electrical and equipment-specific hazards apply independently of the hot work classification. No new policy record. Conditional control C-000393 asks the crew to make the determination. Implementation rule: `SIL-PKG-003-RECONCILED.md` §3.2.

## Package 003 policy relationships (candidate)

- **P-000001 at exposed rotating machinery — decided (SIL PO Decision 006 §1; scoped by Architect Response 005 §4).** For the exposed rotating-machinery operation only (drill press or stationary machine), C-000276 ("Do not wear gloves at an exposed rotating spindle…") presents in place of C-000004. P-000001 continues to apply to handling the steel before and after, to every other step involving steel, and to handheld powered tools. No contradictory glove instruction is presented for the same operation.
- **P-000002** applies to drilling or fastening during demolition, knife use and stainless steel lath. Stainless steel alone does not establish lath. Where demolition or stainless steel lath work also involves an exposed rotating-machinery operation, the safe work method separates material handling (A4 gloves, C-000112) from the machine operation (C-000276) (Architect Response 005 §4).
- **P-000006 to P-000008** — drilling and mechanical fastening are not hot work. Powder- and gas-actuated fastening are not hot work absent an ignition exposure or project requirement. **Hot riveting is hot work** (Architect Response 005 §8). Cumulative where the task also welds, cuts or grinds.
- **P-000009** is not extended to drilling or fastening.
- **P-000010** is not extended to pneumatic, battery-powered or gas-actuated tools. Recognized PAT terms: Hilti gun, Ramset gun, shoot / shot pins (SIL PO Decision 006 §3; Architect Response 005 §5).
