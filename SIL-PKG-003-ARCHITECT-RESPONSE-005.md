# SIL ARCHITECT RESPONSE 005
## Package 003 — Independent Review and Reconciliation Directive

**Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Authority:** Architect  
**Development baseline:** revision-a4 — 7c6ddc6  
**Production baseline:** v1.4.0-p1 — 76ac7c7  
**Disposition:** Accepted in principle, subject to the following corrections and final reconciliation.

## 1. P3-U2 — Canonical record generalizations

Accept all 12 proposed generalizations, subject to the following refinements:

- **H-BT-010:** Contact with or entanglement in rotating tools, bits, cutters, chucks, sockets or extensions. Preserve all existing entanglement relationships.
- **H-BT-002:** Cuts and punctures from sharp metal surfaces, burrs, swarf, pipe ends and flanges.
- **H-BT-021:** Include ejected chuck keys and unsecured attachments explicitly.
- **H-HW-031:** Include uncontrolled workpiece rotation as well as movement, rolling and binding.
- **C-000218:** Require drill-press workpieces to be secured using an appropriate vise, clamp or fixture. Do not imply that hand-holding is acceptable at a drill press merely because the operator believes the workpiece is controllable.
- **C-000224:** Preserve equipment-specific energy isolation requirements, including stored energy and applicable lockout/tagout requirements for servicing stationary machinery.

Accept the remaining proposed generalizations as written.

Preserve all existing identifiers and applicable relationships.

## 2. P3-U3 — Canonical consolidations

Accept the 18 proposed consolidations, including:

- H-DF-004 into H-BT-021.
- H-DF-025 into H-DF-024.
- H-DF-033 into H-DF-028.
- The separation of compressed-air injection from hose whip under H-DF-040 and H-BT-025.

Retired identifiers shall never be reused.

Consolidation must not eliminate method-specific hazards or controls. A shared consequence does not justify consolidation where materially different exposure mechanisms would be lost.

## 3. P3-U4 — Production supersession

Accept the production crosswalk subject to these conditions:

- Retain unresolved generic puncture, hand injury and hand-and-foot injury records until their underlying exposure mechanisms are fully represented.
- Supersede generic eye injury only within the reviewed drilling relationship where the replacement adequately represents the exposure.
- Preserve concrete dust exposure where material composition remains uncertain.
- Accept the proposed equivalent silica-control supersessions.
- Preserve all valid legacy relationships outside the reviewed scope.
- Do not retain contradictory or less protective legacy controls alongside their approved replacements.

Resolve the previously identified duplicate flying-debris suggestions.

## 4. Rotating machinery and glove precedence

Implement PO Decision 006 as approved.

The no-gloves requirement applies to the exposed rotating-machinery operation, not indiscriminately to every step involving steel or powered handheld tools.

Where demolition or stainless steel lath work also triggers P-000002, the safe work method must separate material handling from exposure to rotating machinery.

The A4 requirement remains applicable during the relevant material-handling steps.

Do not present contradictory glove instructions for the same operation.

## 5. Powder-actuated tool recognition

PO Decision 006 contains contradictory wording regarding standalone Ramset recognition.

Apply the more specific clarification:

- Hilti gun: PAT recognition.
- Ramset gun: PAT recognition.
- Shoot pins / shot pins: PAT recognition.
- Standalone Hilti or Ramset: insufficient without applicable fastening context.
- Deck gun: ambiguous without supporting context.

Correct the inconsistency in the current recognition records without altering the preserved historical PO decision.

Additional corrections:

- C-000356: Separate regulatory PAT substrate prohibitions from manufacturer-specific gas-actuated tool restrictions.
- C-000358: Do not imply that manufacturer permission overrides applicable regulatory prohibitions on fastening into spalled areas.
- C-000363: Keep hands clear of the muzzle, fastener path and potential penetration or deflection path.
- C-000365: Preserve the manufacturer's complete misfire procedure without introducing a universal waiting period.
- Gas-actuated nailers must retain their fuel-cell isolation requirements when clearing jams or servicing.

## 6. Silica and material recognition

Accept H-DF-017 and H-DF-018 as separate canonical records.

Required refinements:

- Drilling alone does not establish silica exposure.
- Concrete, masonry, stone and other known or reasonably foreseeable silica-containing materials establish applicable silica relationships.
- Unknown material composition may establish a conditional assessment requirement.
- Drywall and composite materials must not be classified universally as silica-free.
- Wet drilling does not automatically eliminate respiratory protection requirements.
- OSHA Construction Table 1 methods must remain task-specific and jurisdiction-specific.
- Where a specified method requires a respirator, retain that requirement.
- Where the task falls outside an applicable prescribed method, use the required exposure-assessment approach.
- Do not introduce a universal N95 or P100 requirement.

Verify that C-000315 through C-000319 collectively preserve all applicable engineering-control requirements, including dust-collection performance and filter requirements.

## 7. P3-U7 — Adhesive anchors

Accept the mechanical and adhesive anchor consolidation.

Add a conditional control:

"For adhesive anchors installed overhead or upwardly inclined under sustained tension, verify the applicable design, installer qualification and inspection requirements before installation."

Do not impose these specialized requirements on every adhesive anchor.

ACI provisions, adopted code requirements and project specifications require source validation before specific certification or inspection requirements are presented as mandatory.

Maintain P3-U7 as a technical validation item.

## 8. P3-U8 — Hot structural riveting

Accepted.

Heated rivets, rivet forges and hot riveting establish hot work.

Cold riveting, blind riveting and modern mechanical structural fastening do not establish hot work solely because riveting is recognized.

Hot riveting shall also establish applicable thermal-contact, hot-material handling and striking hazards.

Close P3-U8.

## 9. P3-U9 — Electromagnetic-field exposure

Add a new canonical hazard candidate:

**H-DF-041 — Exposure to electromagnetic fields from induction heating equipment.**

Applicability:

- Induction heating.
- High-frequency electromagnetic heating equipment.
- Other equipment where manufacturer instructions identify electromagnetic-field exposure.

Candidate controls:

- Follow manufacturer-established electromagnetic-field precautions and restricted areas.
- Verify applicable restrictions for personnel with implanted or body-worn medical devices before exposure.
- Maintain required separation from energized induction coils and associated equipment.
- Do not bypass protective barriers or equipment safeguards.

Do not establish universal separation distances or medical-device exclusions without equipment-specific validation.

Allocate new permanent control identifiers beginning with C-000394, reusing equivalent controls where available.

## 10. Induction and resistance heating

Correct the proposed classification logic.

Removing nearby combustibles is a hot work control; it is not proof that an ignition-producing process has ceased to be hot work.

The phrases "area cleared of combustibles" and "no combustibles" must not automatically suppress the hot work permit, fire watch or clearance requirements.

Where ignition potential is genuinely absent, hot work classification is not established.

Where ignition potential remains uncertain, retain conditional applicability and C-000393.

Thermal and electrical hazards remain independent of hot work classification.

Do not automatically establish welding fumes, welding-circuit shock, arc radiation, fuel-gas hazards or flashback.

## 11. Compressed-air cleaning

Retain the PO-approved 30 psi threshold in C-000392.

Clarify that:

- The threshold applies to compressed-air cleaning, not pneumatic tool operating pressure.
- Effective chip guarding and appropriate PPE remain required.
- The construction-specific exception must not be presented as a universal exception across all industries.
- Compressed air must never be directed at personnel or used to clean skin or clothing.
- Applicable silica and hazardous-dust cleaning restrictions remain independently enforceable.

Preserve the distinction between regulatory requirements and company policy.

## 12. Additional safety corrections

- **H-DF-015:** Preserve the distinction between post-tensioned tendon energy release and structural weakening from damage to prestressed members. Do not assume identical failure mechanisms.
- **H-DF-026:** Recognize failure of existing life-safety or lifting anchors when inspecting, testing or using them. Suppress installation-specific controls when installation is not occurring.
- **H-DF-029:** Recognize concealed-service exposure independently of whether the substrate is visibly thin.
- **H-DF-039:** Establish battery chemistry and equipment applicability before presenting lithium-ion-specific controls.
- **H-BT-028:** Mandrel collectors apply where the riveter is designed with one.
- **C-000287:** Stop and isolate equipment as necessary before attempting to regain control of a displaced or rotating workpiece.
- **C-000164:** Respirator selection must be based on the contaminant, exposure assessment, atmospheric conditions and applicable respiratory protection program, not solely on ventilation adequacy.

## 13. P3-U10, P3-U11 and P3-U12 — Regulatory validation

These items remain open.

Do not classify a citation as independently verified by the Architect merely because the Engineer has verified it.

Retain the Engineer's existing verification records and their source attribution.

Specifically:

- Validate applicable OSHA Construction, OSHA General Industry and MSHA provisions independently.
- Retrieve and review the actual OSHA gas-actuated fastening-tool interpretation before determining its regulatory training applicability.
- Regardless of regulatory classification, retain appropriate equipment-specific instruction and operator qualification controls.
- Preserve all previously withheld RCSC controls until their outstanding validation requirements are satisfied.

Citation housekeeping alone need not prevent a future test release, but unverified safety-critical instructions shall not be introduced into operational use.

## 14. P3-U13 — Production defects

Accept all 16 identified production defects for correction during the authorized implementation.

Collision handling must be contextual and cumulative.

For example, "drill a beam flange and torque a process pipe flange" must establish both applicable activities without suppressing the legitimate process-piping relationship.

Do not implement global keyword exclusions that eliminate valid activities elsewhere in a mixed task description.
