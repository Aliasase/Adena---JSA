SIL-PKG-004 — Architect Response 002
Accept the reported r2 reconciliation in principle.
Apply the following architectural clarifications:
MSHA Applicability
Replace the generic "At a mine" wording with wording establishing applicability to operations subject to MSHA requirements.
Retain the tag-line requirement for suspended loads requiring steadying or guidance where applicable.
Numerical Safety Requirements
There is no blanket prohibition against numerical safety requirements appearing in the application.
Verified numerical requirements may be included when:

* The applicable operation is correctly recognized.
* The regulatory or technical requirement has been validated.
* Jurisdictional applicability is established.
* The requirement is not incorrectly generalized to unrelated operations.
* The wording provides practical value to the foreman.

Evaluate the verified multiple-lift rigging requirements for inclusion in the applicable controls, including maximum member count, rigging assembly safety factor and minimum member spacing.
Preserve the prohibition against user-facing regulatory citations.
Personnel Hoisting
The steel-erection exception shall not be interpreted as unrestricted authorization to hoist personnel.
Preserve applicable personnel-hoisting safeguards.
Continue withholding the six unvalidated personnel-hoisting controls.
Retain P4-U8 and P4-U9 as unresolved validation items.
