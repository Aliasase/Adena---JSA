# SIL-PKG-001 — ADDENDUM 02
**Hydraulic Bolt Tensioning — Product Owner Scope Confirmation**

The Product Owner has confirmed that Adena utilizes hydraulic bolt tensioners in addition to hydraulic torque wrenches.

Hydraulic bolt tensioning shall therefore be included within Safety Intelligence Library Package 001.

## Recognition Terms

Include the following candidate recognition terms:

- Hydraulic bolt tensioner
- Hydraulic bolt tensioning
- Hydraulic stud tensioner
- Stud tensioning
- Hydraulic stud tensioning
- Axial bolt tensioning
- Hydraulic tensioning pump
- Bolt stretching
- Stud stretching
- Multi-stud tensioning
- Simultaneous bolt tensioning

Generic terms such as tensioning, stretching, pump and tensioner require contextual recognition.

Do not automatically classify hydraulic bolt tensioning as rotational torque application.

## Additional Candidate Hazard Records

**H-BT-033 — Stud or tensioner component ejection under hydraulic pressure**

Controls:
- Verify required thread engagement before pressurizing.
- Keep personnel clear of the axial line of force.
- Verify tensioner compatibility with the stud dimensions and required load.
- Do not exceed manufacturer pressure or piston stroke limits.

**H-BT-034 — Sudden release of stored axial tension**

Controls:
- Release hydraulic pressure in a controlled manner following the specified procedure.
- Verify complete depressurization before removing tensioning equipment.
- Keep personnel clear of potential stored-energy release paths.

**H-BT-035 — Pinch and crush points during tensioner installation and operation**

Controls:
- Keep hands clear of moving pistons, pullers, bridges and nut-turning mechanisms.
- Establish clear communication between personnel positioning tensioners and operating the hydraulic pump.
- Prevent unintended pressurization while positioning or adjusting equipment.

**H-BT-036 — Connection failure from incorrect stud tensioning**

Controls:
- Follow the specified stud tensioning sequence.
- Apply the specified hydraulic pressure and tensioning procedure.
- Complete required verification of the installed connection.
- Use compatible and correctly rated tensioning equipment.
