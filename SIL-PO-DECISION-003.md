## 1. Hand Protection — U-18 Resolved

The existing ANSI A4 minimum cut-resistance requirement applies to:

- All demolition activities.
- Knife use.
- Working with stainless steel lath (expanded metal).

The lath-specific requirement does not automatically extend to galvanized or other expanded-metal products. Appropriate hand protection remains required based on the actual exposure.

Retain P-000002 and C-000112 without expanding the confirmed policy scope.

## 2. Demolition Recognition — U-19 Resolved

The Product Owner accepts the proposed contextual demolition recognition terminology:

- Demo
- Demolish
- Demolition
- Tear out
- Gut
- Wreck
- Selective demolition
- Dismantle structure

Ordinary equipment or component removal shall not automatically establish demolition.

Recognition shall consider the activity, object, conditions and context. When demolition and removal occur together, both may contribute applicable hazard and control relationships.

## 3. Combustible Clearance — P-000006

**Company requirement:** A minimum 35-foot combustible clearance applies to hot work, with greater distances required where applicable.

**Candidate user-facing control:**

Remove combustible materials within 35 feet of hot work. Where removal is not possible, protect them with suitable fire-resistant barriers. Increase the protected area where required.

Applicability shall account for:

- Sparks and slag traveling beyond the immediate work area.
- Elevated hot work.
- Openings in floors and walls.
- Adjacent and concealed spaces.
- Combustible materials on the opposite side of the work surface.

The 35-foot distance is a minimum, not the maximum extent of the exposure assessment.

## 4. Fire Watch — P-000007

**Company requirement:** Fire watch is required during hot work and for a minimum of 30 minutes following completion, with longer durations where required.

**Candidate user-facing control:**

Maintain fire watch during hot work and for at least 30 minutes after completion, or longer where required.

Reconcile the existing production references to 30 minutes and 30–60 minutes into a consistent canonical requirement.

Where additional fire monitoring is required by the project, permit or applicable standard, retain it as a distinct conditional requirement.

Fire watch responsibilities and qualifications will be developed within Package 002.

## 5. Hot Work Permit — P-000008

**Company requirement:** A hot work permit is required for all hot work.

**Candidate user-facing control:**

Obtain and review the required hot work permit before beginning hot work. Comply with all permit conditions.

Do not introduce an exemption for designated welding areas or fabrication shops without explicit Product Owner authorization.

## 6. Policy Precedence — Architectural Clarification

The more protective requirement governs when requirements address the same exposure.

However, requirements addressing different exposure mechanisms are cumulative.

Examples:

- Cut resistance does not replace thermal protection.
- Thermal protection does not automatically establish A4 cut resistance.
- Welding during demolition must account for both applicable hand-protection requirements.
- Chemical, electrical, thermal and mechanical protection must be evaluated independently where exposures coexist.

Update the policy precedence rules accordingly without changing the confirmed company requirements.
