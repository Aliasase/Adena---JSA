SIL-PKG-001 — PRODUCT OWNER DECISION RECORD
1. Fall Protection
The Product Owner confirms that fall protection thresholds are project-specific, subject to applicable regulatory requirements.
Do not establish a universal six-foot threshold or a universal steel erection exception within the safety intelligence library.
User-facing controls shall reflect the applicable project requirements without introducing unsupported numerical thresholds.
2. Mobile Elevating Work Platforms
The Product Owner confirms that personal fall protection and tie-off are required in both boom lifts and scissor lifts.
Controls shall specify manufacturer-approved anchorage points and compatible personal fall protection equipment.
Do not present guardrails alone as sufficient protection for Adena personnel working in scissor lifts.
3. Recognition Terminology
All proposed recognition aliases in the reconciled Package 001 are accepted.
This includes industry terminology, equipment terminology, fastener terminology, abbreviations and brand aliases.
Brand names shall remain recognition aliases and shall not establish mandatory equipment brands in user-facing controls.
Contextual recognition and collision-prevention requirements remain applicable.
4. Glove Requirements
No universal glove cut-level rating has been established through this decision.
Retain task-specific and project-specific hand protection requirements without introducing an unsupported minimum cut rating.
