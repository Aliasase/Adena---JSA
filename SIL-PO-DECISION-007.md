SIL-PO-DECISION-007 — Rigging and Hoisting
Package: SIL-PKG-004
Date: November 26, 2026
Status: Product Owner decision
The Product Owner confirms that Adena has no additional company-specific requirements beyond the proposed defaults for the following:

* Critical-lift classification.
* Written lift plans.
* Wind restrictions.
* Lightning restrictions.
* Rigging inspections.
* Tag-line requirements.
* Lift Director assignments.

Apply applicable regulatory requirements, manufacturer instructions and project/client-specific requirements.
Where multiple applicable requirements address the same exposure, follow the more protective requirement.
Do not establish unsupported numerical thresholds, universal critical-lift definitions or additional Adena-specific policy records.
Preserve all previously approved company policies.
This decision does not authorize implementation or deployment of Package 004.
